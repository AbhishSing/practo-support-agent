# Laboratory Test Turnaround Times

**Document ID:** `KB-006`
**Topic:** `lab-test turnaround times`

---

Standard blood and urine panel results are typically uploaded to the portal within 24 hours of sample collection. Specialized diagnostic tests, such as allergy panels or histopathology, may take between 3 to 5 business days. Critical or abnormal lab results are immediately escalated to the ordering physician for patient follow-up.
