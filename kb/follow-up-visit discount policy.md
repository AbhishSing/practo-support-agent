# Follow-Up Visit Discount Policy

**Document ID:** `KB-010`
**Topic:** `follow-up-visit discount policy`

---

Patients returning for a follow-up consultation within 7 days of their initial visit receive a 50% discount on the doctor fee. Follow-up appointments scheduled between 8 to 14 days post-visit receive a 25% discount. Any consultation occurring after 14 days will be billed at the standard initial consultation rate.
