# Cancellation and Rescheduling Rules

**Document ID:** `KB-002`
**Topic:** `cancellation/rescheduling window`

---

Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled time to avoid a cancellation fee. Late cancellations made within 24 hours will incur a flat penalty charge of ₹500. Patients can easily reschedule their slots online or via our automated WhatsApp assistant.
