# Telemedicine Eligibility Criteria

**Document ID:** `KB-007`
**Topic:** `telemedicine eligibility`

---

Telemedicine visits are available for routine follow-ups, minor General Medicine concerns, skin symptom reviews, and prescription renewals. Initial consultations for acute cardiac issues or severe orthopedic injuries require an in-person examination. Patients must have a stable internet connection and access to a video-enabled camera.
