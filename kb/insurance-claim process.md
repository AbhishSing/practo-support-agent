# Insurance Claim & Cashless Processing

**Document ID:** `KB-004`
**Topic:** `insurance-claim process`

---

We support cashless claims for all major partnered health insurance providers upon presenting a valid health card. For pre-authorized procedures, patients must submit their claim approval documents at least 48 hours prior to admission. Reimbursement claims receive detailed itemized bills and discharge summaries within 3 business days post-visit.
