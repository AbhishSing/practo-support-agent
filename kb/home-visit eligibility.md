# Home-Visit Care Eligibility

**Document ID:** `KB-012`
**Topic:** `home-visit eligibility`

---

Home visit services are reserved for bedridden patients, elderly individuals with mobility constraints, or post-operative recovery care. Services include basic health assessments, blood sample collection, and routine wound dressing by licensed nursing staff. Requests must be placed at least 24 hours in advance and are subject to geographical service zone availability.
