# Consultation Fee Structure

**Document ID:** `KB-003`
**Topic:** `consultation-fee structure by specialty`

---

General Medicine consultations start at ₹500, while specialized visits such as Pediatrics and Dermatology are priced at ₹800. Super-specialties like Cardiology and Orthopedics carry a standard consultation fee of ₹1,200. Diagnostic screenings and minor in-office procedures are billed separately from the base consultation.
